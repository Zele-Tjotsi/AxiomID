# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

AxiomID aims to maintain a professional, respectful, and technically rigorous engineering environment.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully
- critique ideas rather than individuals
- provide constructive technical feedback
- acknowledge mistakes
- document disagreements where appropriate
- prioritize project quality over ego

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following are not acceptable:

- harassment
- discrimination
- personal attacks
- intimidation
- deliberate disruption
- publishing private information without permission
- hostile or abusive communication

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for addressing violations and may remove contributions or restrict participation when necessary to maintain a productive engineering environment.
